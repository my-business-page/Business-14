01 / NOTES ON SPACE

Think about
how a room moves.

Before colour and styling, there is circulation. Before decoration, there is proportion. Before a beautiful photograph, there is a space that has to work every day.

At D'Venue's journal is a set of simple prompts for thinking about interiors with more intention.

NOTE 01

Start with movement

Trace how people enter, pass through, sit, work, store things and leave. The best layout often reveals itself through movement.

NOTE 02

Let materials repeat

A restrained material palette can connect separate zones and make a space feel larger and more coherent.

NOTE 03

Use light as structure

Think beyond brightness. Contrast, shadow and the direction of light can give architecture a daily rhythm.

NOTE 04

Design for tomorrow

A room should not only solve today's brief. Leave enough flexibility for the way life changes.
